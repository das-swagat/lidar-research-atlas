# Governance

The project lead is responsible for scope, releases, legal-risk controls, and final merges. Catalog changes require a primary source, explicit provenance, and review of access and redistribution fields. No contributor may upload third-party data, credentials, model weights, or copied paper figures.

Decisions prioritize: (1) factual accuracy, (2) provider terms and attribution, (3) scholarly usefulness, (4) reproducibility, and (5) visual polish. Popularity never overrides legality or source quality.

# Third-party notices

This repository contains bibliographic facts, independently written summaries, and links to authoritative third-party resources. It does not bundle third-party datasets, labels, weights, paper figures, logos, or source archives.

Names and trademarks identify their respective projects and owners. Each catalog record lists the original contributors, primary publication, official source, implementation relationship, and known license or terms page.

Original diagrams in `docs/assets/images/` explain general concepts and are not reproductions of paper figures. Any future third-party media must be entered in the media register with title, creator, source, license, modifications, and retrieval date before inclusion.

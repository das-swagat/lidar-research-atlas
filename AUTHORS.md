## Third-party authorship

The atlas indexes work created by independent dataset providers, paper authors, and software maintainers. Their authorship is recorded on each catalog page and is not transferred to the atlas maintainers.

## Contributors

Contributors are acknowledged through Git history and release notes. Substantial curatorial or software contributions may be added here with consent.

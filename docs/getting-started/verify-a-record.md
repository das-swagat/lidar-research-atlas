# Verify a record

A record is defensible when its claims can be reconstructed from primary sources.

- Confirm the official landing page and organization.
- Confirm the primary publication, author list, and year.
- Read the current license or data-use terms—not a third-party summary.
- Separate dataset, annotation, code, model-weight, and benchmark terms.
- Confirm whether the linked repository is official, author-maintained, a framework integration, or community code.
- Record the check date and any ambiguity.

Use `partial` rather than filling gaps by inference.

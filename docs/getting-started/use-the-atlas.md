# Use the atlas

## For literature surveys
Filter resources by year, environment, geometry, task, and representation. Treat “commonly evaluated on” as a discovery aid, then verify claims in the original paper.

## For experiments
Select resources whose license, access conditions, sensor data, annotations, and benchmark protocol match the intended work. Freeze a manifest containing source URLs, accepted terms version, download date, subset, and preprocessing fingerprint.

## For teaching
Link students to official pages. Do not distribute instructor copies of restricted data unless the provider explicitly permits it.

## For commercial evaluation
Do not assume that free academic access permits commercial use. Obtain provider or institutional legal review where terms are restrictive or unclear.

# Legal and provenance framework

The atlas is designed to remain useful without becoming a redistribution channel.

- Original providers retain ownership and control of their resources.
- Links point to authoritative access routes.
- Legal summaries are conservative and dated.
- Dataset, annotation, code, weight, documentation, and benchmark terms are separated.
- Unclear status is marked `partial` or `verify`, not inferred.
- Corrections and rights-holder requests receive a documented response.

Read the root `DISCLAIMER.md`, `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`, and policies before publishing a release.
